# Privacy Policy: Craig Hendricks Personal Finance
Last updated: 2026-10-29

"Craig Hendricks Personal Finance" is a personal, non-commercial tool that I, Craig Hendricks, use to look at my own investment accounts. I am its only user. It is not offered to the public, has no customers, and does not handle anyone else's financial information.

## What information is collected
When I connect one of my own accounts through Plaid, the tool receives read-only information about that account: the institution's name, account names and the last digits of account numbers, balances, and investment holdings (what is held, quantities and values). I never see or store my login credentials for my financial institutions; I sign in directly on the institution's page inside Plaid Link.

## How it is used
Only to show me my own portfolio for my own personal tracking. It is not used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions or analytics, and it is not sold, rented or shared with anyone.

## Who provides the data
Plaid Inc. provides the connection to my institutions. Plaid's own handling of data is described in its End User Privacy Policy at https://plaid.com/legal/. You can view and manage connections made through Plaid at https://my.plaid.com.

## Storage, security and retention
The data is kept on a computer I control, protected by disk encryption and access restrictions. Connection tokens are kept only as long as I use a connection (and at most 12 months without use). When I disconnect an account, I delete the stored token and ask Plaid to revoke the connection, and I delete any saved copies of its data.

## Your choices
Because I am the only user, "you" are me; I can disconnect any account at any time. If anyone believes their information has been included in error, they can contact me and I will delete it within 30 days.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above changes.
